Game badge, Z-Crystal and trial stamp artwork
Original graphics: Nintendo / Game Freak / The Pokemon Company.
Sources: Bulbagarden Archives, PokeAPI/sprites and pret game decompilation projects.
These game image assets are not licensed under this application's MIT license.
Native GBA and Platinum sprite/palette atlas frames are extracted losslessly; other PNGs are unchanged.

GSC Kanto goals use HGSS pixel sprites of the corresponding Kanto badge designs.
Diamond/Pearl and BDSP share the Sinnoh badge designs, represented with original Platinum pixel sprites.
Alola rewards remain app collection emblems, represented by their trial Z-Crystals and island trial-completion passport stamps.
Modern badge artwork retains its original pixels and silhouettes; no generic type badge or AI redraw is used.
